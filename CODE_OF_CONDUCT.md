# Code of Conduct

Tolkira follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.

Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks and publishing private information are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block contributors who violate
these expectations. Report concerns privately to the maintainers; do not open a
public issue with personal details.

This is a small project. Keep the bar high for the work and kind for the people.
